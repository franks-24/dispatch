Dispatch delivery policy drafts

Draft for review — this service is not accepting requests. The operator identity, privacy contact, retention schedule and full carriage terms must be approved before launch.

Delivery terms — draft

A quote request is not a binding price. An issued quote must identify its price, included charges, expiry, service and coverage. A collection request requires confirmation before a parcel is handed over. Transit times and carrier availability must be verified for the selected route.

Final terms must define driver eligibility and verification, prohibited goods, packaging, dimensional weight, surcharges, cancellations, loss or damage claims, liability, insurance, delivery evidence, returns and customer support. These draft notes do not establish those terms.

Privacy notice — draft

Quote areas and parcel dimensions are used to assess delivery requirements. Booking addresses, recipient contact details and shipment events are used to arrange and record delivery. Proof of delivery is restricted to authorised access. Driver GPS or live location tracking must not be enabled until its collection purpose, lawful basis, access controls, sharing, retention, and on-duty/off-duty boundaries are documented, reviewed and implemented. Delivery recipients must receive appropriate notice when their details are collected.

Before launch, identify the responsible legal entity and Information Officer, processing grounds, carrier recipients, subprocessors, international transfers, retention periods, access and correction procedures, deletion requests and complaint channels. A privacy acknowledgment does not provide consent for unrelated processing.

Data ownership and permitted use — draft

Submitting content does not transfer ownership to Franks24. The service may process submitted information to provide and administer the requested delivery, subject to the final terms and applicable law. No marketing or AI model training permission is implied by these acknowledgments.
