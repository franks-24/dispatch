You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into these Terms. Google authentication is required. Keep your Google account secure and promptly report suspected misuse through the operator's published support channel. Do not share accounts or impersonate another person.

3. Workers and hiring teams

Workers may create a profile, browse opportunities and submit applications. Hiring teams may register, but cannot publish opportunities until a Dispatch administrator approves the employer account.
