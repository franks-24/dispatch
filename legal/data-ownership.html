Data Ownership & Usage Policy

Your content remains yours. This policy explains the limited permissions Dispatch needs to deliver its services.

Pre-launch legal review required. This draft must be checked against Franks24's registered operating entity, lawful processing practices, support contacts and retention schedule before accepting production users. Last drafted: 30 October 2026.

These documents work together. You will be asked to acknowledge each separately.

1. What stays yours

You retain the intellectual property rights you hold in original profile descriptions, job advertisements, application material and other content you create. Dispatch does not receive ownership of that material merely because you upload or submit it. Employer job materials remain subject to the employer's rights and any third-party rights. Personal information is governed by privacy law, not simply by assertions of ownership.

2. Permission needed to provide the service

By uploading or submitting content, you grant the platform operator a limited, non-exclusive, revocable where legally and operationally feasible, worldwide licence to host, store, process, reproduce and show that content only as required to provide the features you choose, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet lawful obligations. This does not permit unrelated commercial resale.

3. Who can see what

Google identity and profile details support your Dispatch account. Information in an application may be shared with the hiring team responsible for that opportunity. A published job listing may be visible to signed-in eligible users. Administrative personnel and service providers must receive only access justified by their duties, using role-based access controls and audit processes.

4. Responsibility for third-party information

Do not upload personal information about another person unless you have a lawful basis and authority to do so. Hiring teams receiving applicants' information are independently responsible for protecting it and using it for legitimate recruitment, in accordance with applicable law and any contractual data-processing terms.

5. No silent AI training or advertising transfer

This baseline permission does not authorise Dispatch to sell personal information, train general-purpose AI models on your non-public submissions, or repurpose your content for advertising. Such additional uses would require a specific, separately disclosed lawful basis and any necessary opt-in.

6. Exports, correction and removal

Users should be able to request a copy, correction or deletion of their information through the verified privacy contact once published. Removal may be limited by outstanding applications, legal retention duties, fraud-prevention or dispute requirements. The implementation currently has no automated account-export or erasure endpoint; these must be operationalised before launch.

7. Audit records of acceptance

To evidence an informed onboarding choice, Dispatch records separately that you accepted the Terms and acknowledged this Privacy Notice and Data Policy, together with a version and server-generated time. This immutable client-facing record is not itself a claim of ownership. Lawful correction and erasure requests must be handled by the administrator under an approved retention policy.

8. Platform intellectual property

The Dispatch name, interface code, logos and platform materials are owned by their respective rights holders or licensed to the operator; these rights are distinct from a user's contributions. No rights to source code or brand assets are transferred by registering.

9. Policy updates and contact

Material changes affecting content rights will require renewed review and acknowledgment before using protected features. Before production: complete legal entity and contact details, export/deletion procedures, vendor responsibilities, and a published effective date.
