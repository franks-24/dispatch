Privacy Notice

What Dispatch collects, why it is used, who can access it, and how to exercise privacy rights.

Pre-launch legal review required. This draft must be checked against Franks24's registered operating entity, lawful processing practices, support contacts and retention schedule before accepting production users. Last drafted: 30 October 2026.

These documents work together. You will be asked to acknowledge each separately.

1. Who is responsible

Dispatch is presented as a Franks24 service. The registered legal entity acting as the responsible party under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), its Information Officer and official privacy contact must be identified before public launch.

2. What we collect

When you use Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication supplies identifiers such as your unique user ID, email, display name and optional account photo. Onboarding collects your role, name, city or area, and optional phone and skills. Platform activity may include job listings, applications, employer approval statuses, timestamps and security/diagnostic records. We record the policy release and time of your acknowledgments.

3. Purposes and grounds for processing

We use this information to authenticate users, create workspaces, enable job applications and hiring workflows, verify employers, protect users from abuse, troubleshoot issues and comply with legal duties. Processing is based on grounds appropriate to each purpose, including performance of the service contract, statutory obligations, legitimate interests where lawful, or consent where legally required. Acknowledging this Notice is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

4. Sharing and recipients

Google/Firebase service infrastructure processes authentication and application data. Relevant applicant information may be made available to the hiring team for the opportunity they applied to; hiring teams must handle that information lawfully. We do not disclose data to unrelated advertisers as part of the stated MVP. We may disclose information when required by law or to investigate serious abuse, subject to safeguards.

5. Cross-border processing

Cloud infrastructure and service providers may process data outside South Africa. Prior to launch, the responsible party must verify actual Firebase/Google processing locations, applicable contractual safeguards and POPIA section 72 cross-border transfer conditions. Do not treat a selected Firestore region as a guarantee that all personal information stays there.

6. Retention and deletion

Keep personal information only as long as necessary for operational, legal, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution needs. The precise retention periods for profiles, applications, logs and acceptance evidence must be published after approval of a retention schedule. Requests to erase data are evaluated against applicable legal retention obligations; the online UI currently has no self-service deletion feature.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or objection to certain uses of your information; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; or lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator. A verified privacy request channel and response procedure must be in place before production launch. You may stop onboarding without submitting a profile.

8. Security

Google sign-in and Firebase Security Rules help constrain access. Additional controls include least-privilege administration, access reviews, encrypted transport, monitoring and incident response. No internet service is perfectly secure. A production incident response and breach notification process must be confirmed before launch.

9. Cookies, analytics and marketing

Authentication may use browser storage and cookies necessary for sessions. This onboarding flow has no bundled marketing consent. Any optional analytics, marketing, non-essential cookies or AI model training on user-provided content require separate disclosure and, where required, a separate opt-in.

10. Changes and contact

Material changes will be communicated and may require renewed acknowledgment. Before launch: add the official responsible party, Information Officer/privacy contact, verified hosting regions, detailed retention schedule, subprocessors, and an effective date after legal and operational approval.
