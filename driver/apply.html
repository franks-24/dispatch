Identity and eligibility

Prepare a readable identity document, evidence of your right to work, a clear profile photo and contact details. Verification is completed privately by authorized staff.

Photo and document tips

Use good light, show all document corners, avoid glare and keep text legible. Never post documents in public chats. A profile photo is not a biometric identity verification.

Licence, vehicle and insurance

Prepare the licence appropriate to your vehicle. A reviewer determines permit applicability.

For motor vehicles, prepare registration/licence-disc evidence, permission if you are not the owner, safety inspection evidence and insurer confirmation of courier use. Cargo cover and limits must be confirmed separately.

South African Government: professional driving permits
Safety screening

Franks24 requires a human safety and risk review. Staff will confirm the approved screening provider, purpose, lawful processing basis, costs, validity and private submission method before a check is commissioned.

Results, corrections and re-checks

A pending case is not a conviction. Ask support for a private review or correction of inaccurate results. Reviewers assess relevant evidence; this checklist never clears a criminal record or makes an automatic rejection. Re-check dates follow the approved Franks24 policy and evidence expiry.

Do not submit fingerprints or criminal-record details in this preview.

Agreement and payout readiness

Before accepting, review the Franks24 courier agreement, privacy notice, commission, VAT treatment, deductions, payout cycle and dispute process. No Uber terms, rates or package limits are adopted by this checklist.

Signed records must be archived privately in the configured Franks24 dispatcher Google Drive. An archive is confirmed only after a successful save; staff must resolve failures before treating it as complete.

What happens next

01 · Apply

Use your own verified Google account and submit basic contact and vehicle information.

02 · Verify

Reviewers separately verify identity, eligibility, licence/permit, vehicle, insurance, safety and payout evidence using restricted channels.

03 · Accept & approve

Only approved drivers can see their own dispatch offers, trips and recorded earnings. No job use while driving.
